QR Code — Primary Call-to-Action

Both ads use the QR code as the sole call-to-action. There is no "Register your interest" button. The QR is labelled "Scan QR code for more" (gold, uppercase) so that readers unfamiliar with QR codes understand what to do. It links to https://bdefarmtrac.co.uk.

The version embedded in the HTML files is low-resolution (screen only). For print, regenerate the QR code at high resolution using any free QR generator (e.g. qr-code-generator.com) — export as SVG or PNG at 300 dpi minimum, pointing to https://bdefarmtrac.co.uk.
