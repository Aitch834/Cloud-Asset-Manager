## BDE Farm Trac: Viticulture Module
### Submitted by BDE Farm Trac — approx. 490 words

**Every block, every spray, every vintage**
*How one platform is helping UK vineyards stay audit-ready without the paperwork mountain*

---

The administration that comes with growing grapes in Britain has never been more demanding. A single season now generates spray diary entries for every block, phenology observations, harvest chemistry records, must analysis data, disease scouting logs and — for those pursuing PDO or PGI status — a steadily thickening stack of provenance documentation. Add excise duty records for any wine produced on site and the paperwork alone can feel like a second job.

For most growers, the answer has been a collection of spreadsheets, paper notebooks and disconnected apps. It works, after a fashion — until the Red Tractor inspector arrives, a certifying body requests three years of spray applications for a specific block by variety, or HMRC asks for a reconciliation of fruit harvested against wine produced.

BDE Farm Trac, developed specifically for British agriculture, brings all of this together in a single platform with a dedicated viticulture module. The idea is straightforward: every block, every application, every chemistry reading and every harvest figure lives in one place, accessible from a smartphone in the middle of the vineyard or a desktop in the winery office.

The vine register sits at the core. Each block carries its own permanent record — variety, clone, rootstock, planting density, training system and area — with photographs and seasonal notes attached directly to the entry. Phenology events are logged against individual blocks throughout the season, building a year-on-year picture of development that supports both management decisions and the detailed records required for PDO and PGI certification.

Spray diary entries are linked to each block and capture product, dose rate, water volume, operator name, EAMU reference and re-entry interval in a format designed to satisfy Red Tractor and organic audits without transcription or reformatting. Disease scouting observations — botrytis pressure, downy or powdery mildew incidence, vine moth trap counts — are recorded at block level and timestamped, providing the evidence trail that certifying bodies and premium buyers increasingly expect to see.

At harvest, the platform captures Brix, total acidity, pH and yield by block, with winery handover notes stored alongside the fruit data. For producers with WOWGB excise duty obligations, harvest tonnages feed directly into the duty records, reducing the reconciliation workload at year end.

Everything works on mobile — a practical necessity for vineyards where the work happens outside, often with limited connectivity.

"The aim was to make compliance a by-product of good record-keeping, not a separate exercise," says the BDE Farm Trac team. "If you're logging your sprays and recording your harvests accurately, you should be audit-ready as a matter of course — without any extra effort."

For UK vineyards navigating an increasingly scrutinised regulatory environment, that ambition is well timed.
